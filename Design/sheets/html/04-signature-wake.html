4.1

Why it is the signature

Real operators read gauges by their behaviour as much as by their value: a steady needle, a needle that climbs and settles, a needle that will not sit still. The Wake draws that behaviour. It is the same on every dial, it needs no sound and no text, and it turns the hardest thing to teach, cavitation, into a shape.

It also solves reduced motion for free. The flutter is decoration; the wake is the information. With motion off, the needle holds still and the wake still shows exactly how unsettled it was.

Three shapes to learn

SteadyA sliver. Water is in balance; leave it alone.
SurgeA fading trail on one side. Something changed; watch it settle.
HuntingA fan on both sides. Supply is failing or the pump is cavitating.
