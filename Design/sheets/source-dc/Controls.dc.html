Delivery valve

Quarter-turn lever. Drag the coloured grip through 90°; detents at closed and open, holds anywhere between. Across the pipe = closed. A tap does nothing, so a stray thumb never charges a line.

Throttle · vernier

Side view of a push-pull T-handle. Drag it out for coarse travel; the graduated shaft is revealed as you pull. − / + are the vernier twist, 25 rpm a step. Released, it stays put: a cable, not a spring.

3.4

Primer

Press and hold. The ring fills as vacuum builds and the cap sinks 2 px. Lets go on its own when the pump catches, or after 30 s, the point at which a real operator would suspect an air leak.

3.5

Warning lamp

Glass lens in a galv bezel. The severity shape appears only when lit: triangle = caution, steady; diamond = alarm, flashing 2 Hz. Tap a lit lamp to acknowledge: it stops flashing and opens the coaching card.
