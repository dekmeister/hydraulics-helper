One delivery, three places

Colour lets a sighted player trace panel to scene in one glance. Glyph and numeral carry the same identity for everyone else: under protan/deutan simulation, Gum and Galah converge in hue, so the shape is what separates them. Triangle and diamond are never delivery glyphs: they belong to the caution and alarm signals.
